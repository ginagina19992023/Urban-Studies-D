[Sassen · core idea] From the 1980s factories moved to low-wage countries and production spread across the world. Sassen's point is that the more dispersed production becomes, the more complex it is to manage, and the management concentrates: cross-border finance, contracts, tax, accounting, advertising and consulting cluster in a few cities. New York, London and Tokyo become the command points of the global economy.

【Sassen · 核心意思】全球化让生产分散了，控制反而集中了。1980 年代起工厂搬到工资低的国家，生产在全世界铺开；但生产越分散，管理越复杂。跨国融资、合同、税务、会计、广告、咨询这些"管理全球"的高端生产性服务业（advanced producer services），集中到少数几个城市。纽约、伦敦、东京成了全球经济的"指挥中心"（command points）。

[Sassen · cities and nations] Global cities become more tightly linked to each other than to the rest of their own country; London's ties to New York may be closer than its ties to northern England. This is what the slide means by globalization as a saviour of the autonomy of cities.

【Sassen · 城市与国家】全球城市彼此之间的联系，比和本国其他城市的联系还紧密：伦敦和纽约的关系，可能比伦敦和英国北部工业城市更近。讲义说全球化是城市自主性的"拯救者"，指的就是城市在全球网络里有了自己的位置。

[Sassen · inequality] Employment polarizes. At the top, highly paid finance, law and consulting professionals; at the bottom, low-paid jobs that serve them (cleaning, security, food, delivery, care), often done by migrants; the stable middle-income jobs of manufacturing shrink. High earners push up central housing costs, which links Sassen to the course's housing and gentrification units.

【Sassen · 为什么不平等】就业两极分化：上层是高薪的金融、法律、咨询专业人士；下层是为他们服务的低薪工作（清洁、保安、餐饮、送货、照护），很多由移民承担；制造业带来的稳定中等收入工作随工厂外迁而萎缩。高收入人群推高市中心房价和租金，这就接上了课程后面的住房商品化和绅士化。

[Sassen · Sydney] Sydney is often seen as Australia's closest thing to a global city, with finance and professional services concentrated in the CBD. Zanardo et al. (2024) note that the Waterloo area is seen as insufficiently dense for its proximity to the Sydney CBD, and that redevelopment would add up to 6,000 private apartments: land near the command centre is treated as too valuable for public housing.

【Sassen · 悉尼】悉尼常被视为澳大利亚最接近全球城市的城市，金融和专业服务集中在中央商务区（CBD）。Zanardo 等（2024）写到，Waterloo 一带被认为"相对于其靠近悉尼 CBD 的位置，密度不足"，再开发将增加多达 6000 套私人公寓。靠近"指挥中心"的土地，被当作太值钱、不该用来住公屋租户。

Key works: Edward Soja, Postmodern Geographies (1989); Mike Davis, City of Quartz (1990), especially the chapter "Fortress L.A."; Michael Dear & Steven Flusty, "Postmodern urbanism", Annals of the AAG 88(1) (1998); Michael Dear (ed.), From Chicago to L.A. 拓展Sisson 2024 ↗ 原文段落· §5.2 人口

Burgess 模型用"入侵与演替"把分层说成自然过程；Sisson 分析的"劣势集中"话语同样把聚集说成问题的原因，而忽略了是分配政策把最弱势的人集中进了公屋。

[Chicago model] Burgess pictured the city growing outward from one centre in rings: the Loop (CBD), a zone in transition where new immigrants and the poor first settle, workers' homes, middle-class residences, and an outer commuter zone. Groups move outward as they rise, through "invasion and succession", an analogy borrowed from plant ecology. This is why the slides say the Chicago School naturalized the unequal city: sorting by income looks like a natural ecological process rather than the result of institutions.

【芝加哥模型】Burgess 设想城市从一个中心向外一圈圈生长：中心商务区（Loop）、新移民和穷人最先落脚的过渡区、工人住宅区、中产住宅区、最外层的通勤区。群体地位上升后就往外搬，即"入侵与演替"（invasion and succession），这是从植物生态学借来的类比。讲义说芝加哥学派把不平等的城市"自然化"了，原因就在这里：按收入分层看起来像自然的生态过程，而不是制度造成的结果。

[Core claim] Los Angeles has no dominant centre. Dear & Flusty argue that the periphery organises the centre, not the other way round; they describe development as "keno capitalism", where parcels on a grid are developed seemingly at random rather than spreading out in rings.

【核心主张】洛杉矶没有主导性的中心。Dear & Flusty 认为是外围在组织中心，而不是中心组织外围；他们把这种开发方式称为"基诺资本主义"（keno capitalism）：网格上的地块像彩票开奖一样看似随机地被开发，而不是一圈圈向外扩散。

[fragmented] The city is cut into disconnected pieces, socially and administratively. Los Angeles County is made up of many separate municipalities, each governing itself.

【fragmented 碎片化】城市被切成互不相连的小块，社会上、行政上都是。洛杉矶县由许多独立的市组成，各管各的。

[polycentric] There is not one downtown but several functional centres; business and employment are spread across the region, the "edge city" pattern.

【polycentric 多中心】不止一个市中心，而是多个功能中心，商务区和就业区散布各地，即"边缘城市"（edge city）。

[decentralized] Development spreads outward and the suburbs no longer depend on the centre; a sprawling city tied together by freeways.

【decentralized 去中心化】发展向外扩散，郊区不再依附市中心，是一座由高速公路连起来的蔓延式城市。

[multi-ethnic] A city of immigrants in which ethnic enclaves sit side by side, such as Koreatown and Little Tokyo. The Koreatown case in DeVerteuil's typology is here.

【multi-ethnic 多族裔】由移民构成，族裔聚居区并存，如韩国城、小东京。课上 DeVerteuil 类型学里的韩国城案例就在这里。
