0.0 · From the slides — Origins and schools of thought0.0 · 讲义原文梳理 · 学科起源与几大流派

Engels (1844): The Condition of the Working-Class in England: the miserable conditions of the industrial city (life expectancy 26 years) prompted the first impulse to study cities, responding through mapping and action, together with an anti-urban bias.

Engels (1844)：《英国工人阶级状况》：工业城市的悲惨条件（平均寿命 26 岁）促成最早的城市研究冲动，通过制图和行动回应城市问题，也伴随一种"反城市偏见"（anti-urban bias）。

Chicago School (from the 1920s): Urban development is driven by competition for space and the choices of individuals; defined the American city as unequal and segregated.

芝加哥学派（1920s 起）：城市发展由空间竞争与个体选择驱动，把美国城市定义为不平等、隔离的。

1970s political-economy critique: Competition and individual choice are not characteristics of "the city" but of a specific city, the capitalist city. The Chicago School naturalized the unequal city, which suited the political elite. Spatial inequality comes from the politics of the state and city, the power of landowners and the profit-seeking of developers.

1970 年代政治经济学批评：竞争和个人选择不是"城市"的特征，而是"资本主义城市"的特征；芝加哥学派把不平等的城市"自然化"了，客观上服务于政治精英。空间不平等来自国家与城市的政治、地主的权力、开发商的逐利。

Los Angeles School: A separate slide listing urban form: fragmented, polycentric, decentralized, multi-ethnic, controlled, fortified.

洛杉矶学派：讲义单独一页，只列城市形态特征：碎片化（fragmented）、多中心（polycentric）、去中心化（decentralized）、多族裔（multi-ethnic）、受控（controlled）、堡垒化（fortified）。

Weber (1921): The European city as a unique social and political entity: the market, its own laws, autonomy, an association of urban residents. Contrasted with Asian cities.

Weber (1921)：欧洲城市是独特的社会政治实体：市场、自身的法律、自治、市民的某种联合体，用以与亚洲城市对照。

Sassen (1991): Global cities such as New York, London and Tokyo become hubs that manage, coordinate and drive globalization, and are also sites of deep inequality and social transformation.

Sassen (1991)：纽约、伦敦、东京等全球城市成为管理、协调、驱动全球化的枢纽，同时也是深刻不平等与社会转型的场所。

Brenner & Schmid (2021): Planetary urbanism: urbanization is a global process driven by capitalism, neoliberalism and globalization, occurring at urban and rural scales. It is a "terrain of struggle" over land, housing, environmental justice and access to basic services, so the agency of social groups matters.

Brenner & Schmid (2021)：星球城市化：城市化是由资本主义、新自由主义、全球化驱动的全球过程，发生在城乡各种尺度上；城市化是围绕土地、住房、环境正义、基本服务准入的"terrain of struggle"，要理解各社会群体的能动性。

Correction An earlier version attributed the "state, landowners, developers" passage to the Los Angeles School. In the slides these are two separate pages and are now listed separately.

更正早先版本把"国家、地主、开发商"那段 1970 年代批评归给了洛杉矶学派。讲义里两者是分开的两页，现已拆开。

0.0b · Beyond the slides — The Los Angeles School and the Chicago model it answers0.0b · 讲义外补充 · 洛杉矶学派，以及它回应的芝加哥模型

[Note] The slides list only six words for the Los Angeles School. This section adds background from the literature; it is not slide content. Check the original works before citing.

【说明】讲义对洛杉矶学派只列了六个词。这一节是根据相关文献补充的背景，不是讲义原文，写进论文前请核对原著。

[Who] In the 1980s–90s a group of scholars working in Los Angeles (UCLA, USC) argued that the Chicago School model no longer fitted, and that Los Angeles showed what the contemporary city looks like. Key works: Edward Soja, Postmodern Geographies (1989); Mike Davis, City of Quartz (1990), especially the chapter "Fortress L.A."; Michael Dear & Steven Flusty, "Postmodern urbanism", Annals of the AAG 88(1) (1998); Michael Dear (ed.), From Chicago to L.A. (2002).

【是谁】1980–90 年代，一批在洛杉矶（UCLA、USC）工作的学者提出：芝加哥学派的城市模型已经不适用，洛杉矶才代表当代城市的样子。代表著作：Edward Soja《Postmodern Geographies》（1989）；Mike Davis《City of Quartz》（1990），尤其是"Fortress L.A."一章；Michael Dear & Steven Flusty《Postmodern urbanism》，Annals of the AAG 88(1)（1998）；Michael Dear（编）《From Chicago to L.A.》（2002）。

[Chicago model] Burgess pictured the city growing outward from one centre in rings: the Loop (CBD), a zone in transition where new immigrants and the poor first settle, workers' homes, middle-class residences, and an outer commuter zone. Groups move outward as they rise, through "invasion and succession", an analogy borrowed from plant ecology. This is why the slides say the Chicago School naturalized the unequal city: sorting by income looks like a natural ecological process rather than the result of institutions.

【芝加哥模型】Burgess 设想城市从一个中心向外一圈圈生长：中心商务区（Loop）、新移民和穷人最先落脚的过渡区、工人住宅区、中产住宅区、最外层的通勤区。群体地位上升后就往外搬，即"入侵与演替"（invasion and succession），这是从植物生态学借来的类比。讲义说芝加哥学派把不平等的城市"自然化"了，原因就在这里：按收入分层看起来像自然的生态过程，而不是制度造成的结果。

[Core claim] Los Angeles has no dominant centre. Dear & Flusty argue that the periphery organises the centre, not the other way round; they describe development as "keno capitalism", where parcels on a grid are developed seemingly at random rather than spreading out in rings.

【核心主张】洛杉矶没有主导性的中心。Dear & Flusty 认为是外围在组织中心，而不是中心组织外围；他们把这种开发方式称为"基诺资本主义"（keno capitalism）：网格上的地块像彩票开奖一样看似随机地被开发，而不是一圈圈向外扩散。

[fragmented] The city is cut into disconnected pieces, socially and administratively. Los Angeles County is made up of many separate municipalities, each governing itself.

【fragmented 碎片化】城市被切成互不相连的小块，社会上、行政上都是。洛杉矶县由许多独立的市组成，各管各的。

[polycentric] There is not one downtown but several functional centres; business and employment are spread across the region, the "edge city" pattern.

【polycentric 多中心】不止一个市中心，而是多个功能中心，商务区和就业区散布各地，即"边缘城市"（edge city）。

[decentralized] Development spreads outward and the suburbs no longer depend on the centre; a sprawling city tied together by freeways.

【decentralized 去中心化】发展向外扩散，郊区不再依附市中心，是一座由高速公路连起来的蔓延式城市。

[multi-ethnic] A city of immigrants in which ethnic enclaves sit side by side, such as Koreatown and Little Tokyo. The Koreatown case in DeVerteuil's typology is here.

【multi-ethnic 多族裔】由移民构成，族裔聚居区并存，如韩国城、小东京。课上 DeVerteuil 类型学里的韩国城案例就在这里。

[controlled] Space is surveilled and privately governed: homeowners' associations, private security, cameras.

【controlled 受控】空间被监控、被私人化管理：业主协会、私人保安、监控摄像。

[fortified] People are separated by physical means: gated communities, defensive architecture, privatised "public" space. Mike Davis argued that urban space was increasingly designed to keep unwanted people out.

【fortified 堡垒化】用物理手段把人隔开：门禁社区、防御性建筑、被私有化的"公共"空间。Mike Davis 认为，城市空间越来越被设计成把"不受欢迎的人"挡在外面。

[vs the 1970s critique] The 1970s political-economy critique asks who causes inequality (the state, landowners, developers, capital); the Los Angeles School describes what the resulting city looks like (fragments, many centres, fortification). They do not contradict each other, and several LA School members, such as Soja and Davis, were themselves Marxist. Read in order, the three slides go: the Chicago School naturalizes inequality → the 1970s critique says institutions produce it → the LA School shows the shape of the city that results.

【和 1970 年代批评的区别】1970 年代政治经济学批评问的是"不平等是谁造成的"（国家、地主、开发商、资本）；洛杉矶学派描述的是"结果城市长成什么样"（碎片、多中心、堡垒化）。两者不矛盾，Soja、Davis 等成员本身也是马克思主义取向。讲义三页按顺序读：芝加哥学派把不平等自然化 → 1970 年代学者指出不平等由制度造成 → 洛杉矶学派描述这种城市最后的空间形态。

[Criticism] The main criticism of the LA School mirrors its own criticism of Chicago: it treats the features of one city as a universal model of the city.

【主要批评】学界对洛杉矶学派的主要批评，和它批评芝加哥学派的理由一样：把一座城市的特征当成了所有城市的普遍模式。

[Links to your material] Nanjing (Song et al. 2023): most highly gentrified quarters are gated communities, and the authors' "club-isation" of landscape is a Chinese version of fortified and controlled. Kern (2007): condos turn security into a purchasable commodity, the same logic. Hangzhou: Qianjiang New Town, Binjiang, Future Sci-Tech City and Xiaoshan existing side by side is a ready example of polycentric and decentralized.

【和你手上材料的联系】南京（宋伟轩等 2023）：高度绅士化小区多为封闭社区，作者说的"空间俱乐部化"就是 fortified 和 controlled 的中国版本。Kern（2007）：公寓把"安全"变成可以买的商品，也是同一逻辑。杭州：钱江新城、滨江、未来科技城、萧山并存，是 polycentric 和 decentralized 的现成例子。

0.1 · Simmel (1903) — The Metropolis and Mental Life0.1 · Simmel (1903) · The Metropolis and Mental Life 大都市与精神生活

Notes: Research-Strategy/Draft-Simmel-1903.md

笔记：Research-Strategy/Draft-Simmel-1903.md

[Core argument] City life changes people at the psychological level. The pace, density and complexity of the metropolis produce a distinct mental attitude.

【核心论点】城市生活从心理层面深刻改变人：大都市的快节奏、高密度、复杂性塑造出一种独特的心理状态。

[Key concepts] Rural relationships are few, deep and built over time; metropolitan ones are many, shallow and anonymous, so city dwellers come to place their trust in money and exchange. The blasé attitude: emotional detachment as self-protection against overstimulation.

【关键概念】乡村关系少而深、靠时间积累；大都市关系多而浅、大量匿名，城市居民转而与货币和交换建立信任。Blasé attitude（冷漠态度）：面对过度刺激，以情感疏离作为自我保护。

0.2 · Wirth (1938) — Urbanism as a Way of Life0.2 · Wirth (1938) · Urbanism as a Way of Life 都市主义作为一种生活方式

Notes: Research-Strategy/Draft-Wirth-1938.md

笔记：Research-Strategy/Draft-Wirth-1938.md

[Core argument] Three structural variables, size, density and heterogeneity, explain five features of urbanism as a distinct way of life.

【核心论点】用规模、密度、异质性三个结构变量，解释都市主义作为一种独特生活方式的五个特征。

[Five features] Anonymity and impersonal relationships

【五大特征】匿名性与非个人化关系

[Five features] Specialization and division of labour (economic interdependence)

【五大特征】专业化与劳动分工（经济互赖）

[Five features] Cultural diversity (promotes tolerance, may also cause fragmentation and conflict)

【五大特征】文化多样性（促进宽容，也可能导致碎片化和冲突）

[Five features] Rational, calculated, goal-oriented behaviour (individualism over community ties)

【五大特征】行为理性化、目标导向（以社群纽带换取个人主义）

[Five features] Weakened social ties

【五大特征】社会纽带弱化

[vs Simmel] Simmel focuses on psychological mechanism (stimulation → blasé); Wirth on social structure (size + density + heterogeneity → anonymity, division of labour, weak ties). Much Chicago School research builds on accounts like Wirth's.

【与 Simmel】Simmel 偏心理机制（刺激 → blasé），Wirth 偏社会结构（规模 + 密度 + 异质性 → 匿名、分工、纽带弱化）。芝加哥学派的城市研究很大程度建立在这类论述之上。

0.3 · Sennett (2018; slides 2019) — Building and Dwelling: Ethics for the City0.3 · Sennett (2018 / 讲义 2019) · Building and Dwelling: Ethics for the City

Notes: Research-Strategy/Draft-Sennett-2019.md

笔记：Research-Strategy/Draft-Sennett-2019.md

[Core argument] There is a tension between building (the physical construction of cities) and dwelling (how people experience and live in them). Better cities balance the technical side of planning with residents' lived experience.

【核心论点】城市的物质建设（building）与人们如何体验和生活其中（dwelling）之间存在张力；更好的城市要平衡规划设计的技术面与居民的真实生活经验。

[Key definition] A city is "a human settlement in which strangers are likely to meet". Cities should have public spaces where residents acquire urban competence by learning to socialize with strangers.

【关键定义】城市是 "a human settlement in which strangers are likely to meet"。城市应提供公共空间，让居民通过与陌生人交往习得 urban competence（都市能力）。

2.0 · Lecture framework — Eight points2.0 · 讲义框架 · 八个要点

[① What is housing] A basic need (who should provide it?), a commodity from which to extract wealth, or a way of life?

【① 住房是什么】基本需求（谁该提供？）、榨取财富的商品，还是一种生活方式？

[① What is housing] "Today there is a conflict between housing as lived, social space and housing as an instrument for profitmaking – a conflict between home and real estate." (Madden & Marcuse 2016, 4)

【① 住房是什么】"Today there is a conflict between housing as lived, social space and housing as an instrument for profitmaking – a conflict between home and real estate."（Madden & Marcuse 2016, 4）

[② Financialization] Aalbers (2016): the increasing role of financial motives, markets and actors in shaping housing. Actors include institutional and international investors, listed and non-listed real-estate investment companies (REITs) and foundations.

【芬兰】远看：隔离水平在欧洲和北欧都偏低，缺乏负面邻里效应；赫尔辛基自 1970 年代起实行小尺度社会混合，依托庞大的社会住房部门。

[Finland] Up close: in the era of market-oriented housing production, segregation has risen across Europe, Finland included; in Finland's largest cities it has grown steadily, challenging the welfare-state ethos.

【芬兰】近看：市场导向的住房生产时代，包括芬兰在内的欧洲国家隔离上升；芬兰最大城市的隔离近几十年持续增长，挑战福利国家理念。

[Finland] From proactive housing policy to reactive social policy: area-based initiatives spread nationwide in the 1990s; supply-side subsidies gave way to demand-side ones; in the 2010s the social housing stock declined and private renting grew, criticized as accelerating financialization; municipalities cannot influence tenant selection in market housing.

【芬兰】从主动住房政策转向被动社会政策：1990 年代区域干预全国推广；供给侧补贴被需求侧取代；2010 年代社会住房存量下降、私人租赁增长，被批评加速了金融化；市政府无法影响市场化住房的租客选择。

[Finland] A retrenching regime: since 2024 housing benefits have been cut along with other social security; retrenchment began in the 1980s; Kynsilehto & Alastalo (2024) call it a " post-welfare state ", with segregation delegated to local levels and responsibility for housing markets increasingly shared with the private sector.

【芬兰】收缩中的住房体制：2024 年起住房补贴随其他社保一起削减；收缩始于 1980 年代；Kynsilehto & Alastalo (2024) 称之为"post-welfare state"，隔离等社会问题被下放到地方，住房市场的责任越来越多地与私人部门分担。

1 · Shaw (2008) — Gentrification: What It Is, Why It Is, and What Can Be Done about It1 · Shaw (2008) · Gentrification: What It Is, Why It Is, and What Can Be Done about It

[Core argument] Gentrification is a continuum of social and economic geographic change, from outright disinvestment to super-gentrification. There is no need to assume every neighbourhood passes through every stage.

【核心论点】绅士化是一个社会与经济地理变化的连续谱，从彻底撤资到 super-gentrification，不必预设所有社区都走完阶段模型。

[Key concepts] Ruth Glass (1964) coined the term for the "gentry" moving into working-class London and renovating old houses

【关键概念】Ruth Glass (1964) 首创这个词，指"绅士"涌入伦敦工人阶级社区、买下并翻新老房子

[Key concepts] Neil Smith stresses its class character: working-class neighbourhoods "rehabilitated" by middle-class buyers, landlords and developers

【关键概念】Neil Smith 强调阶级性质：工人阶级社区被中产购房者、房东和开发商"修复"

[Key concepts] Rent gap: when the gap between capitalized and potential ground rent is wide enough, redevelopment pays and the area is "ripe"

【关键概念】Rent gap：当前资本化地租与潜在地租的差距足够大时，开发有利可图，社区"成熟"到可以被绅士化

[Key concepts] Three waves (Hackworth & Smith 2001): sporadic and state-funded → market-driven and resisted → corporate capital with systemic state involvement; Lees et al. propose a fourth wave (financialization + pro-gentrification policy)

【关键概念】三波绅士化（Hackworth & Smith 2001）：零星、政府资助 → 市场驱动、伴随抵抗 → 大资本主导、国家系统性介入；Lees et al. 提出第四波（金融化 + 亲绅士化政策）

[Key concepts] The displacement debate: Tom Slater argues that gentrification research has itself been gentrified, no longer attending to displacement

【关键概念】置换争议：Tom Slater 批评"绅士化研究本身已经被绅士化"，研究者不再关注置换

[Policy] Gentrification advances fastest where new investment is least needed. The best time to intervene is before the rent gap fully opens, when protecting low-income areas and investing in public facilities is most feasible economically and politically.

【政策启示】绅士化在最不需要新投资的地方推进最快；介入的最佳时机是租差尚未完全打开之前，此时保护低收入社区、投资公共设施在经济和政治上都最可行。

2 · Davidson (2018) — New-build gentrification (Handbook of Gentrification Studies, ch. 15)2 · Davidson (2018) · New-build gentrification （绅士化研究手册第 15 章）

[Core argument] New-build gentrification (new condo towers, waterfront developments) has become the dominant form of third-wave gentrification, often actively backed by the state: state-led gentrification.

【核心论点】新建绅士化（新建公寓塔楼、滨水开发）已成为第三波绅士化的主导形式，常有国家积极支持，即 state-led gentrification。

[Key concepts] Davidson & Lees (2005), four elements: reinvestment of capital, social upgrading, landscape change, direct or indirect displacement

【关键概念】Davidson & Lees (2005) 四要素：资本再投资、社会阶层升级、景观变化、直接或间接置换

[Key concepts] The residentialization debate: Lambert & Boddy argue new-build without direct displacement is not gentrification; Davidson & Lees reply with indirect displacement (the area becomes unaffordable, existing shops and services disappear)

【关键概念】Residentialization 之争：Lambert & Boddy 认为没有直接置换就不算绅士化；Davidson & Lees 以间接置换反驳（社区变得难以负担、原有商店服务消失）

[Key concepts] Reurbanization: a European term for population returning to the core that sidesteps class displacement; the author argues it hides the class dimension

【关键概念】Reurbanization：欧洲学者用来描述人口回流市中心、回避阶级置换的说法，作者认为它掩盖了阶级维度

[Key concepts] Global new-build gentrification takes different state–capital forms in China (state-led, large-scale demolition), the UK, Sweden, the Philippines and elsewhere

【关键概念】全球新建绅士化：中国（国家主导、大规模拆迁）、英国、瑞典、菲律宾等地的国家与资本关系各不相同

[Quotable] "gentrification without displacement is not gentrification". Indirect displacement and the pressure created by social-mix policy are often overlooked.

【可引用】"gentrification without displacement is not gentrification"。但间接置换、social mix 政策造成的驱逐压力常被忽视。

[Core argument] The relationship between immigration and gentrification has long been neglected and is more complex than "immigrants get displaced". Five types:

【核心论点】移民与绅士化的关系长期被忽视，远比"移民被驱逐"复杂。提出五类关系：

[Five types] Immigrants as a barrier to gentrification (Goutte d'Or, Paris)

【五种类型】移民作为绅士化的屏障（巴黎 Goutte d'Or）

[Five types] Immigrants and gentrifiers coexisting (bubble model, Stoke Newington, London)

【五种类型】移民与绅士化者并存（bubble model，伦敦 Stoke Newington）

【五种类型】移民被驱逐（经典案例，唐人街）

【五种类型】移民本身成为绅士化者（洛杉矶韩国城，移民企业家再投资族裔飞地）

4 · Lees (2008) — Gentrification and Social Mixing: Towards an Inclusive Urban Renaissance?4 · Lees (2008) · Gentrification and Social Mixing: Towards an Inclusive Urban Renaissance?

[Core argument] Governments in the UK, US and Netherlands present social-mix policy as the positive face of gentrification: moving middle-class residents into poor areas to reduce segregation and build "community capital". The evidence does not support this logic.

【核心论点】英、美、荷政府把 social mix 政策当作绅士化的正面包装：把中产搬进贫困社区以减少隔离、提升"社区资本"。证据显示这套逻辑站不住。

[Six critiques] Gentrification produces displacement, segregation and polarization, not integration

【六大批判】绅士化造成置换、隔离、两极化，而不是融合

[Six critiques] Gentrifiers do not really interact with low-income residents; their daily paths rarely cross

【六大批判】绅士化者并不真正与低收入居民交往，生活轨迹几乎不交叉

[Six critiques] Social mix is one-way: affluent areas are never asked to take in the poor

【六大批判】social mix 是单向策略，从不要求富裕社区接纳穷人

[Six critiques] It destroys the mutual-support networks that clustering itself provides

【六大批判】它摧毁了贫困聚居本身带来的互助网络

[Six critiques] Policy never says how much mix is enough

【六大批判】政策从未说清多少混合才算够

[Six critiques] Policy never explains how mixing happens; putting people side by side does not produce it

【六大批判】政策从未说明混合如何实现，把人放在一起不会自动产生混合

[Quotable] "gentrification is part of an aggressive, revanchist ideology designed to retake the inner city for the middle classes" (citing Neil Smith)

【可引用】"gentrification is part of an aggressive, revanchist ideology designed to retake the inner city for the middle classes"（引 Neil Smith）

5 · Yrjänä (2026) — By any means necessary: the anti-segregation efforts and state-led gentrification in Helsinki5 · Yrjänä (2026) · By any means necessary: the anti-segregation efforts and state-led gentrification in Helsinki

Cities 170, 106718 · open access · full text: External-Sources/Yrjana-2026-Helsinki-State-led-Gentrification.pdf

Cities 170, 106718 · 开放获取 · 全文：External-Sources/Yrjana-2026-Helsinki-State-led-Gentrification.pdf

[Core argument] Helsinki's social-mix policy, pursued in the name of anti-segregation, amounts to state-led gentrification: a depoliticized, morally justified statistical discourse legitimizes the state's push for market dominance in housing and land.

【核心论点】赫尔辛基以"反隔离"为名推行的 social mix 政策，实质是国家主导绅士化：用一套去政治化、道德正当的统计话语，为国家在住房和土地市场里加强市场支配提供合法性。

[Case] Stigmatized eastern suburbs Mellunkylä and Meri-Rastila. The 2020 "new urban renewal model" targets four suburban renewal areas, aiming to deconcentrate disadvantaged and ethnic-minority groups and raise owner-occupation and the social-rental share.

【案例】赫尔辛基东部被污名化的外围社区 Mellunkylä 和 Meri-Rastila。2020 年启动"新城市更新模式"，聚焦 4 个外围更新区，目标是分散弱势群体与族裔少数群体、提高自住率与社会租赁份额。

[Theoretical tools] Reputational gap (Kallin 2017): the distance between a place's imagined potential and its stigmatized present; the wider the gap, the more justified intervention appears

【理论工具】声誉差距（reputational gap，Kallin 2017）：地方被想象的潜力与被污名化的现状之间的差距，差距越大，干预越显得正当

[Theoretical tools] Subsidy gap (Gray & Kallin 2023): the state first absorbs the costs of land assembly and acquisition to de-risk private capital

【理论工具】补贴差距（subsidy gap，Gray & Kallin 2023）：国家先垫付土地整备、征收等成本，为私人资本去风险

[Theoretical tools] Social mix as corrective: decontextualized statistics frame the area as a "problem", justifying demolition and densification

【理论工具】social mix 作为矫正措施：用去语境化的统计描述把社区塑造成"问题"，为拆除和加密提供正当性

[Theoretical tools] False choice urbanism (Slater 2014): the only alternative to gentrification is continued decline

【理论工具】虚假选择城市主义（false choice urbanism，Slater 2014）：除了绅士化只剩持续衰败

[Theoretical tools] Green gentrification (Wallin 2021): upgrading parks and public space to attract investment. A planner: "If you think about it cynically, building this park is partly about raising the image of the area"

【理论工具】绿色绅士化（Wallin 2021）：翻新公园和公共空间吸引投资。规划官员原话："如果你从犬儒角度想，建这个公园某种程度上就是为了提升这片区域的形象"

[Theoretical tools] Managed decline (Watt 2023): deliberate under-maintenance in anticipation of demolition

【理论工具】管理式衰败（managed decline，Watt 2023）：拆除预期下故意不维护

[Theoretical tools] Socializing risk, privatizing profit (Risager 2022)

【理论工具】社会化风险、私有化利润（Risager 2022）

[Fieldwork] 2024–2025: repairs stalled while rents rose (+8.4% in 2024, +5.3% in 2025); older residents worried about heating and snow clearing; families unable to plan schooling around uncertain demolition timelines; residents learned of demolition from the local paper; the "right to return" does not guarantee the original flat; the Kontula shopping-centre renewal deadlocked for 20 years, with developer NCC withdrawing in March 2025.

【田野证据】2024–2025 年调研：维修停滞但租金照涨（2024 年 +8.4%，2025 年 +5.3%）；老人担心暖气和清雪；家庭因拆迁时间线不确定无法规划孩子上学；居民从本地报纸得知自家楼要拆；"重返权"不保证原公寓；Kontula 购物中心更新僵局 20 年，开发商 NCC 2025 年 3 月退出。

[Conclusion] Helsinki wants both deeper private dominance of the housing market and the prevention of segregation, and manages the contradiction through gentrification disguised as anti-segregation discourse (Lees 2008). The author suggests treating access to affordable housing as a normal need rather than a social-policy problem; with its planning monopoly and extensive land ownership, Helsinki could prioritize state-subsidized rental housing.

【结论】赫尔辛基既想深化住房市场的私人主导，又想预防隔离，于是采用了"伪装成反隔离话语的绅士化"（Lees 2008）。作者建议把获得可负担住房当作正常需求而非社会政策问题；赫尔辛基有规划垄断权和大量土地所有权，完全有能力优先发展国家补贴租赁住房。
