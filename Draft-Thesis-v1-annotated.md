# 论文草稿 v1：每一段对应课件的哪一部分

## 2. The global city: why Waterloo is drawn into the dispute

### ¶2.1

The introductory unit of the course places Sydney’s housing in the frame of the global city, a city whose competitiveness rests on central land, infrastructure and the services of a global economy (Sassen 1991). The state’s metropolitan strategy of 2014 follows this logic. It defines a Global Economic Corridor that “generates over 41 per cent of the NSW Gross State Product” and names Green Square among its strategic centres, while the text of the strategy does not name Waterloo (NSW Planning and Environment 2014, 44). The unit also treats urban space as a terrain on which claims on land are contested, and Wynne (2018) shows what this means for tenants in Waterloo, who live inside a policy of global-city ambition that reaches their homes. I read land as the place where the ambition of the city meets the people who already live on it. This applies the unit’s concepts to Sydney, which the lectures do not do.

> 课件：导论单元。概念：Sassen 的全球城市；斗争场域（讲义里 Brenner & Schmid 的说法）。展开：用 2014 年规划把概念落到悉尼，说明为什么这片土地被需要；最后一句标明“土地成为雄心与居民相遇的地方”是应用，不是讲义的话。证据：规划文本、Wynne (2018)。

### ¶2.2

Prices give this ambition its weight. The median transfer price of attached dwellings in Sydney, the market in which flats such as Waterloo’s sit, rose from $390,000 in June 2006 to $840,000 in June 2026, and that of houses from $495,000 to $1,487,600 (Australian Bureau of Statistics 2026; Figure 1). The rise has paused. Cotality’s index shows Sydney values down 9.4 per cent over the year to October 2026, although they remain 34.5 per cent higher than ten years earlier (Cotality 2026). New tenants in postcode 2017, which includes Waterloo, paid a median of $1,050 a week in the June 2026 quarter, against $780 for Greater Sydney (NSW Department of Communities and Justice 2026). The picture is not uniform. Census median rents in Waterloo were $520 in 2016 and $530 in 2021, and in Zetland they fell from $650 to $600, although the two censuses are not strictly comparable (Australian Bureau of Statistics 2016a, 2016b, 2021a, 2021b).

> 课件：导论单元，也为绅士化单元的租金差做铺垫。展开：用价格数据说明土地为什么值钱；同时写出 2026 年回调和 2016–2021 年的反例，不写成“现在还在涨”。注意：邮编 2017 对应 Waterloo 是常识，文件没有写明，需核对。证据：ABS、Cotality、DCJ、普查。

### ¶2.3

Within this setting the metro station became the trigger for change. The urban design study for Waterloo South calls it “the catalyst for the renewal” (Turner Studio & Turf 2020, Part 1, PDF p. 6), and the planning proposal argues that keeping the site as it is would ignore “the potential catalytic effects of the new Metro station” (NSW Land and Housing Corporation [LAHC] 2020, 92). The estate itself is read through a diagnosis that the state applies to estates in general. Future Directions finds that “many estates experience high levels of crime, unemployment, domestic violence, tenancy management problems, poor educational outcomes and associated child protection issues” (NSW Government 2016, PDF p. 24). The frame explains why the land is wanted. The diagnosis, taken up in section 4, explains why the housing on it is thought to need replacing.

> 课件：导论单元收尾，转向隔离单元。展开：地铁站是触发点；官方对住宅区的诊断是后面第 4 节要检验的对象。证据：规划建议书、城市设计研究、Future Directions（全州文件，不是 Waterloo 专属）。

## 3. Housing: the strategy, and the conflict between home and real estate

### ¶3.1

The housing unit asks what housing is for. Its central contrast is between housing as a lived, social space and housing as an instrument of profit, a conflict between home and real estate (Madden and Marcuse 2016). The strategy for Waterloo shows both sides. In December 2015 the state announced that the estate would be demolished and rebuilt with about 30 per cent social and 70 per cent private housing, the sale of the private dwellings funding the project (Zanardo et al. 2024, 1208), in line with a statewide target of 70:30 (NSW Government 2016, PDF p. 9). The Premier said that no one would want tenants “sitting in run down 1950s-style accommodation” (Gerathy 2015). In November 2019 the planning route changed from a state-led process to a council-led one (Turner Studio & Turf 2020, Part 1, PDF p. 6), and in March 2021 the state took the role of planning authority from the council (NSW Department of Planning and Environment 2022, PDF p. 11). The planning proposal describes the existing properties as “at the end of their economic life” (LAHC 2020, 23), while the same study gives their average age as 44 years against a benchmark life of 60 (Turner Studio & Turf 2020, Part 1, PDF p. 6).

> 课件：住房单元。概念：Madden & Marcuse 的家与房地产。展开：先给出讲义的对立，再按时间写 2015–2021 年策略怎样变，并列出官方对房子的描述（Baird、经济寿命）。注意“44 年对 60 年”并排出现，文件没有解释。证据：Zanardo 等、Future Directions、规划文件。

### ¶3.2

The terms then changed (Figure 2). The plan approved in 2022 provides 3,012 dwellings, of which 847 are social housing (NSW Department of Planning and Environment 2022, PDF p. 37). After a change of government in March 2023, the share of social and affordable housing across the estate was raised from 34 to 50 per cent, tenants were promised the right to return, and relocation was to proceed in small stages (NSW Government 2023). In 2024 the government promised at least six months’ notice and dedicated support, and repeated that all residents would be given the right to return (NSW Government 2024). By 2026 a new concept application proposed about 3,300 homes, at least half of them social or affordable (Scully 2026), and fencing began around the first block of about 150 homes, due for demolition by the end of June (Bowring 2026). Seventy social homes at the Waterloo Metro Quarter have been occupied (NSW Government 2025). Descriptions of the estate did not soften with the change of government. Brad Hazzard, a minister under the former government, called it “terrible”, and Rose Jackson, a minister under the present one, “not fit for purpose” (Zanardo et al. 2024, 1208–1209).

> 课件：住房单元（政策与策略的变化）。展开：2022–2026 年条件在变（比例、回迁、通知），而对住宅区的负面描述跨两届政府连续。证据：规划部报告、新闻稿、Zanardo 等转引。这里显示的是承诺和提案，不是结果。

### ¶3.3

Read through the unit, the strategy places the estate between the two sides. On the side of real estate, the planning proposal states that the existing controls “would not be capable of supporting a feasible redevelopment of the entire site given the costs of demolition and construction” (LAHC 2020, 92), an early proposal offered extra floor space only if 30 per cent of residential floor area were social housing and 20 per cent affordable (NSW Land and Housing Corporation 2021, Attachment A), and the sale of private dwellings carries the cost. On the side of home, there are the right to return, more social homes in Waterloo South than there are now (Homes NSW 2026), and at least 15 per cent of new social and affordable homes for Aboriginal people (NSW Government 2023). No official document sets out the funding model in these terms. The reading is an application of the unit’s contrast.

> 课件：住房单元（家 vs 房地产）。展开：商品一边和使用一边各举证据，最后一句说明这是对讲义对立的应用，没有官方文件直接说明土地价值资助模型。

## 4. Segregation: testing the remedy against the chain

### ¶4.1

The segregation unit gives the course’s account of how segregation reproduces itself. Tammaru et al. (2021) describe vicious circles of segregation as the product of the connectedness of social and spatial inequalities in different life domains: advantage breeds advantage and disadvantage breeds disadvantage. The lecture sets out five steps (Figure 3). Disadvantage clusters spatially, neighbourhood effects follow, poor perceptions shape who moves, segregation spreads to other domains such as schools and work, and it becomes immobility across the life course and across generations (Rosengren 2026). The state’s case for renewal follows the same chain. Future Directions finds disadvantage concentrated in estates, says that its effects “can be passed on through multiple generations, reinforcing the cycle of disadvantage” (NSW Government 2016, PDF p. 24), and proposes a ratio of 70:30 of private to social housing “to enable more integrated communities” (NSW Government 2016, PDF p. 9).

> 课件：隔离单元。概念：Tammaru 等的恶性循环；Rosengren 的五步链。展开：先写讲义里的链，再指出官方的更新理由就是这条链，药方是混合租住。这是全文的中心，后面四段按步骤检验。

### ¶4.2

The renewal acts most clearly on the first step. Waterloo South now has 749 social and 120 private dwellings, about 86 per cent social housing, and the approved plan has 847 social dwellings among 3,012, about 28 per cent (NSW Department of Planning and Environment 2022, PDF pp. 8, 37; shares calculated from these figures; Figure 4). This is the dilution by social mixing that the lecture names as the way residential segregation becomes less visible. The precedent that the urban design study uses for density says less about mix. Green Square Town Centre is tabulated with 104 social and affordable dwellings among 3,308, about 3 per cent, and Waterloo and Zetland together are shown rising from 143 to 247 people per hectare (Turner Studio & Turf 2020, App. 6, 463–464). The comparison is limited, because the town centre is one part of Green Square and the City’s own target is 7.5 per cent social and 7.5 per cent affordable rental housing across its area (City of Sydney n.d.).

> 课件：隔离单元第 1 步（弱势聚集），及讲义里“被小尺度社会混合稀释”的说法。展开：用计划数字写占比 86%→28%（占比是算术），再用 Green Square 先例的 3% 说明混合的程度。限定：3% 是一个镇中心；表格来自扫描件，需对照原页。

### ¶4.3

Where displaced households go decides whether a cluster is dissolved or moved. Current tenants have been relocated to other public housing, including at least 70 into the new homes beside the Waterloo metro station (Bowring 2026). The government names two local destinations, the 70 social homes at the Metro Quarter and about 95 more at Elizabeth Street, Redfern (Homes NSW 2026), and in 2023 it presented Elizabeth Street as a way for Waterloo South tenants to be relocated in the local area (NSW Government 2023; Figure 5). In the chain’s terms, these place social housing together in separate buildings nearby. The documents do not give the number of tenants at each destination. What can be said is that the first step is diluted on one site while social housing is placed on others.

> 课件：隔离单元第 1 步和第 3 步（谁移动、移到哪里）。展开：被搬走的人去了其他社会住房，是把聚集搬了地方的证据。限定：只有去向的名称，没有各去向的人数，所以只能写“可以说的是……”。

### ¶4.4

The later steps rest on evidence that is less settled. Sisson (2024) notes that the concept of neighbourhood effects behind “concentrated disadvantage” “controversially” places the cause in the estate, and Zanardo et al. (2024) conclude that most of the existing buildings, with the possible exception of the slab blocks, could have their lives extended with relatively modest renovation. Tenure mix changes who lives where. It does not by itself change services, schooling or employment, which steps four and five concern. Meanwhile uncertainty already acts on steps three and five. Wynne and Rogers (2021) argue that the threat of eviction “has already done significant damage to their relationship to place” before any move. A resident, Julie, said that she heard of the demolition work when she woke to find fences going up, and that her relocation had been delayed, and another, Karyn Brown, said of the estate, “It is very functional” (Bowring 2026). The planning minister’s release says that the renewal “stalled for years” (Scully 2026). Read through the chain, residents neither choose nor time their mobility, and the wait is part of the circle. This is a reading. The documents do not measure residents’ mobility or opportunities.

> 课件：隔离单元第 2–5 步；并连到在地失所（Wynne & Rogers）。展开：邻里效应有争议（Sisson）、建筑质量不一（Zanardo），混合租住处理不到服务、学校、就业；不确定性作用于第 3、5 步。限定：这是读法，没有测量。

## 5. Gentrification: price, cost and promise

### ¶5.1

The gentrification unit asks who gains when land is reinvested in and who bears the cost. Smith’s rent gap is the disparity between potential ground rent and the rent capitalised under the present use (Smith 1979), and Stein describes gentrification as “planned by the state, produced by the developers and consumed by the condo crowd” (Stein 2019, 41). Zanardo et al. (2024) connect Waterloo to a gap between the ground rents realised by the state and those potentially received through partial privatisation and redevelopment, and Sisson (2024) shows that the Communities Plus program selects high-value sites so that the state can obtain “value for money”. Sydney’s council states that gentrification “has exacerbated relative inequality among those who can and cannot afford housing” and that the market is becoming “virtually inaccessible to those on very low to moderate incomes” (City of Sydney n.d.). For Sydney and Melbourne, Atkinson et al. (2011, 10) found that the displacement rate from gentrified neighbourhoods was around 50 per cent higher than the out-movement rate for equivalent households elsewhere, although their data come from the 2000s.

> 课件：绅士化单元。概念：Smith 租金差；Stein 的国家主导（引自讲义）。展开：把价格背景连到“谁获得收益”，并用 City of Sydney 自己的文件和 AHURI 2011 说明代价。限定：AHURI 数据早于更新。

### ¶5.2

The unit’s critique of social mixing bears on the remedy. Lees (2008) argues that policy treats gentrification as a route to more mixed and less segregated communities on a poor evidence base, and describes social mixing as a positive packaging of gentrification. Yrjänä (2026) shows in Helsinki how an anti-segregation policy can serve state-led gentrification, with a subsidy gap in which the state pays the difference to de-risk private investment. Waterloo’s documents use the language of mixing, and Homes NSW states that “part of the success of mixed housing communities is that social and private housing are indistinguishable” (Homes NSW 2026). The planning documents also cite Green Square as a precedent for built form (LAHC 2020, §5.1.7), and its town centre has about 3 per cent social and affordable housing (Turner Studio & Turf 2020, App. 6, 463). Whether a mix of 28 per cent social housing, within a market-led development, dilutes the cluster or prepares the area for higher-income residents is the question of the unit. The documents describe the intention and do not yet show the result.

> 课件：绅士化单元。概念：Lees 对社会混合的批评；Yrjänä 的国家主导绅士化。展开：用文献的论点读 Waterloo 的“混合”；Green Square 先例出现在这里（支线）。限定：这是文献的论点用来读案例，不是发现。

### ¶5.3

Protections have grown on paper since 2015. They include the right to return, six months’ notice, staged relocation, more social homes and a share for Aboriginal households (NSW Government 2023, 2024; Homes NSW 2026). The reported gaps are also clear. The plan replaces 749 social dwellings with 847, an increase of 98 (NSW Department of Planning and Environment 2022, PDF pp. 8, 37; difference calculated), the minister could not confirm how much of the 20 per cent affordable housing would be permanent (Bowring 2026), and activists said that at least ten people were still living on the site while the minister said that the remaining people were not tenants (Bowring 2026). No figures are published on how many tenants return or how soon. For the gentrification unit the question is therefore not whether the new buildings will exist but who will live in them and near them in ten years’ time. The documents promise return and do not yet show it.

> 课件：绅士化单元（置换：直接与间接）。展开：纸面上的保护多于 2015 年；记录到的缺口；回迁没有数据。限定：新闻里的说法要写成“据报道”；增加 98 套是计算差；间接置换在这两个片区没有找到研究。

## 6. Conclusion and discussion

### ¶6.1

The renewal changes the first step of the chain by diluting the social housing on the site. Whether it breaks the circle depends on three things that the documents leave open: where the displaced tenants live, how many return, and what the market around the estate does. On the evidence assembled here, the renewal places some tenants in other social housing nearby and promises the return of the rest, in a market where gentrification has widened the gap between households in private housing and in public housing estates (City of Sydney n.d.). The reputational gap that Zanardo et al. (2024) and Sisson (2024) describe, a devalued present set against an idealised future, offers one explanation for why the remedy is accepted. The planning documents point to precincts such as Green Square for the future they offer (LAHC 2020, §5.1.7). That reading is mine, and it is the least tested part of the argument.

> 课件：收回隔离与绅士化两个单元。展开：回答论文问题——“取决于去向、回迁和周围的市场”，不是“已经打破”或“只是搬走”；声誉差距与先例作为支线放在这里。

### ¶6.2

The argument has limits. It rests on documents and published studies rather than interviews, so residents’ experience is represented through other people’s research and single news reports. Relocation destinations are named but not counted. The price series turned in 2026, so the claim concerns two decades of rise and not the present. The five-step chain is a way of reading the case, and the documents do not use it. No official document sets out the land-value funding model, and the rents of Waterloo and Zetland did not rise between the 2016 and 2021 censuses.

> 课件：边界说明（对应课程要求的 discussion）。把限定一次说清：无访谈、去向无人数、价格 2026 年转向、五步链是读法、土地价值模型无官方说明、普查租金反例。

### ¶6.3

Several things follow for planning, and each can be checked against the documents. The return promise should be defined and reported: how many tenants return, when, and to the same place or only the same area. The share of social housing should be published alongside the number of social homes, because the number rises while the share falls. The destinations of relocated tenants should be reported. The permanence of the affordable homes should be confirmed. Where the existing buildings can be renovated, retention should be considered (Zanardo et al. 2024). These suggestions do not require that the policy acts in bad faith. They ask that the cost of the remedy to the people it is meant to help be counted.

> 课件：讨论部分，连回四个单元：回迁（绅士化单元）、占比与去向（隔离单元）、保留与翻新（住房单元）。每一条都可以对照文件检验。
