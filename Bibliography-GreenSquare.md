## 与 Waterloo 的连接（最重要）
- ✅ Wynne, L. *Trying to stay local in Sydney's 'global arc': social housing tenant perspectives*（University of Tasmania；会议论文，年份与会议名待核对）。UTS 存档版：<https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/bitstream/10453/133423/1/apo-nid178396-1090201.docx>（APO 页面对机器人返回 403，已读的是存档 docx）
  - “Waterloo sits within this global economic corridor. It is also part of an ‘urban renewal corridor’ and sits alongside the Central-to-Eveleigh ‘global city precinct’ and the Green Square ‘strategic centre’.”（转引 NSW《A Plan for Growing Sydney》2014，规划原文待核对）
  - “Surrounded by gentrified areas, Waterloo's public housing now comprises one of very few pockets of low-income housing remaining in the inner city.”
