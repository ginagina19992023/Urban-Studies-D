# 悉尼其他公屋区的更新：与 Waterloo 对照的证据

整理日期：2026-11-09。"已核对"指我在下载的原文里逐句找到了该句。

## 已核对的条目
- **Sisson & Ruming (2024/2025)，Housing Studies 40(8)，p. 1825：** "while the total number of dwellings increased on these sites, the number of social housing dwellings on-site decreased, with net increases in housing stock being in private tenures"。
- **同文 p. 1839（Waterloo）：** "Waterloo Central and North currently comprise 1,263 public housing dwellings and, under the plans of the previous NSW Government, would be redeveloped into approximately 4,200 dwellings, 30% of which would be social housing. This would deliver approximately 1,260 gross social housing and a net social housing loss of three dwellings. The renewal of the entire Waterloo estate would thus deliver only 148 net social housing and proportional social housing uplift of just 7%."（前政府的计划）
- **Bonnyrigg，Pinnegar, Liu & Randolph，Bonnyrigg Longitudinal Panel Study First Wave（UNSW City Futures，2012 年田野调查）：** 900 多户，90% 以上住公屋；"had become increasingly stigmatised, suffered high levels of crime"；去集中化的逻辑包括 "diluting"；离开的家庭 "All resettled in non-estate, suburban settings where public housing is not the majority tenure"；这部分只有 7 个访谈，约占已离开的 160 多户的 5%，"only be taken as indicative rather than representative"；离开者 "felt that they had been forgotten by the system"。
- **AHURI Final Report 412：** "Darcy and Rogers (2019) trace the 70:30 ratio back to the Bonnyrigg suburban expansion in NSW (early 2000s) and similarly found that social-mixing outcomes was not a determinant of decision-making."（p. 77）
- **AHURI Final Report 413（Porter 等 2023）：** 维多利亚的参照：Carlton 约 40% 居民回迁，但他们先被安置到其他住宅区或零散购买的房子；Kensington 只有 20% 回迁。这两个是维多利亚的数字，不是悉尼的。
- **Airds-Bradbury，Jaques 等 (2017)：** 1,540 套住宅，94% 由政府持有和管理。
- **Claymore，Campbelltown 市议会 (2018)：** 约 1,151 套住宅，98% 为公共所有；Radburn 设计 "has resulted in poor amenity"。
